quick reference

sound
Keys are the 16 voices. The last one you tap is the one write mode edits. Hold a key to erase its line.
write
Keys are the 16 steps. Tap for a hit, again for an accent, again for off. Hold to clear the line.
pattern
Tap to queue the next pattern. Tap chain, then tap patterns in order, then chain again to run it. Hold a key to drop the chain.
fx
Keys 1–8, active only while held: low, high, crush, two stutters, echo, drop drums, drop synth.
rec
With rec on and the sequencer running, keys you play in sound mode are written to the nearest step.
track
Flips drum and synth. On synth, tap the line at the top of the screen to change scale, double-tap for the root note. It starts in c major, like the original.
knobs
Tone and decay belong to the selected voice, like knob A and B on the hardware — every drum keeps its own pair. Tempo, swing and volume are global.
disk
Four save slots plus a copy-paste code. Everything autosaves as you go.
keyboard
1234 qwer asdf zxcv play the pad, space starts and stops. Sideways gives you the 8×2 layout.
